# NASA challenge alignment audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-13
**Scope:** Read-only implementation and browser review; no application, dataset, dependency, or service changes.

## Source confidence

The [official challenge page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/) was not directly retrievable in the audit environment. Its indexed official summary confirms an interactive “SI-powered” dashboard that summarizes, ranks, and interprets NASA microgravity-combustion findings for fire-safety insights in human space exploration. The official video captions returned empty text; no video claim is attributed here. The finer-grained requirements below are either derived from scientific usability or taken from the secondary statement reproduction recorded in [Step 2](02-requirements-matrix.md); they are not represented as independently confirmed official-page wording. “SI-powered” is not evidence of a requirement for general superintelligence or a specified model.

## Alignment conclusion

The central summarize-rank-interpret workflow is implemented and demonstrable. The project is not a fire-safety certification system and does not contain direct partial-gravity combustion evidence. Detection/suppression objective controls do not imply that all three domains have equivalent ingested outcome records. Overall submission readiness remains **PARTIAL** until source-count wording and demo discoverability are improved and manual accessibility checks are completed.
